Changes
- Prefetch the next two images using the actual card dimensions and matching Scale.FILL.
- AsyncImage replaces subcomposition on the review path.
- Reveal a prepared next card under the drag; advance when the outgoing card clears the viewport.
- Material motion springs preserve release velocity and return cancelled gestures.
- Keep/Delete buttons use the same animation and ignore duplicate requests during handoff.
- Progressive theme-color tint and large native check/trash badges follow the moving card.
- Feedback distinguishes Kept, Added to Delete List, and Marked for deletion.
- Threshold feedback occurs once per gesture; confirmation occurs at release/button commit.
- Platform haptics honor the app toggle and system settings, with guarded older-API fallbacks.
- Background MediaStore/resume refresh preserves the active deck and appends new media.
